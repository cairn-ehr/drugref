# drugref — the DrugCentral `ddi` ingest (issue #101)

**Date:** 2026-08-23 · **Status:** design, approved · **Sequencing:** ROADMAP § 5c — the next **content**
slice, after the re-measurement that made it designable.

The measurement this design rests on is
[`drugcentral-ddi-remeasurement-results`](2026-08-23-drugref-drugcentral-ddi-remeasurement-results.md),
regenerated by `tools/drugcentral_ddi_spike.py` rather than transcribed. Every figure below that carries a
number is either taken from that file or measured here and marked as new. **Nothing in this design may be
quoted forward without re-deriving it** — that instruction is what the re-measurement round exists to teach,
and seven figures decayed for want of it.

## 2. Rule 6, and why the determination gets a runtime guard

DrugCentral publishes the compilation under **CC BY-SA 4.0** — re-confirmed at source on 2026-08-23 — **which
is not evidence of a right to relicense a third-party compendium inside it.** Every `ddi` row cites one of
three references, read out of the dump's own `reference` table rather than inferred:

| `ddi_ref_id` | rows | what the dump says it is | rule 6 |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `2` | 7,571 | Veterans Health Administration (VHA) National Drug File - Reference Terminology (NDF-RT) | **clean — bundle** |
| `3` | 37 | Lexicomp Online — Wolters Kluwer Health | **out** |
| `1` | 13 | *Stockley's Drug Interactions* — Karen Baxter, ISBN 0853699143, 2010 | **out** |

**`BUNDLEABLE_REF_IDS = {2}` has exactly one home in the code.** The re-measurement's own review found a
second hard-coded `ref_id == "2"` in the renderer, unconnected to the set that filtered the rows, so this is
not a hypothetical failure.

**And the constant alone is not enough.** `2` is a surrogate key in a dump that has been published once. A
re-publication is free to renumber its references, and a silent renumber would bundle Lexicomp under a
constant that still reads `2`. So the orchestrator additionally **reads the `reference` row for every admitted
id and aborts the ingest unless its recorded identity matches**:

```
id 2 → authors 'Veterans Health Administration'
       title   'Veterans Health Administration (VHA) National Drug File - Reference Terminology (NDF-RT)'
```

A mismatch is a hard abort with the two strings printed, not a warning and not a skip. Licensing is a blocker,
not a cleanup item, and this is the one place in the slice where being wrong is unrecoverable after
distribution.

## 3. Two measurements this design adds, neither of which is in the re-measurement

Both were taken on 2026-08-23 off the cached extract of the recorded dump
(SHA-256 `055904d152d6c8eef4ee872b25f6476019682df8b5f49bcdf7cc018204f3e04f`), over the bundleable 7,571 rows.
They are stated here because each one decided a section below.

### 3.1 The `description` column carries no clinical content

All **7,571 of 7,571** descriptions match `NAME1/NAME2 [VA Drug Interaction]` — 35 characters at the shortest,
75 at the longest, mean 46, and every one contains the `/` separator:

```
ERYTHROMYCIN/MIDAZOLAM [VA Drug Interaction]
AMITRIPTYLINE/LINEZOLID [VA Drug Interaction]
INDINAVIR SULFATE/SIROLIMUS [VA Drug Interaction]
```

Issue #101's *"Every row carries a description"* is true and **empty**: there is no mechanism, no management
and no prose of any kind. The content DrugCentral adds over a bare pair list is **one severity band, and
nothing else** — which is why §5 spends its whole design budget on the band and none on text extraction.

The label is not worthless, though, and §5.2 stores it: it names the endpoints at **product/salt grain**
(`INDINAVIR SULFATE`, `PIOGLITAZONE HCL`) while `drug_class1`/`drug_class2` carry the base, and that is the
only visible explanation for §3.2.

### 3.2 The source asserts an UNORDERED pair, and 4 pairs disagree with themselves

| measure | value |
|---|---:|
| rows (`ddi_ref_id = 2`) | 7,571 |
| distinct **ordered** endpoint-name pairs | 7,571 |
| ordered pairs appearing more than once | **0** |
| distinct **unordered** endpoint-name pairs | 7,538 |
| pairs present in **both** orders | **33** |
| of those, with a different `description` | **33** |
| of those, with a different `ddi_risk` | **4** |

```
gatifloxacin + pioglitazone  → Critical      GATIFLOXACIN/PIOGLITAZONE HCL [VA Drug Interaction]
pioglitazone + gatifloxacin  → Significant   GATIFLOXACIN/PIOGLITAZONE     [VA Drug Interaction]
```

The two orientations are **two VA entries at different salt grains**, folded onto one base-name pair. So
orientation carries no meaning in this source, and the 33 duplicates are not noise to be dropped but a
genuine intra-source disagreement that has to resolve **deterministically**.

**This is what rules `moiety_contraindication` out** (§4.1): db/014 documents it as *"DIRECTIONAL: the subject
is the drug the statement is ABOUT, and swapping the columns changes the meaning."* DrugCentral asserts no
subject. Storing it there would fabricate an orientation — 7,534 rows of it.

## 4. The shape

### 4.1 What was rejected, and why it is worth recording

- **Widening `moiety_contraindication`.** Smallest migration; reuses the table built for exact pairs. Rejected
  on §3.2 (it is directional and DrugCentral is not) and on severity: that table's whole comment says the
  candidate tier carries no grade, and its `relationship` CHECK admits only `CI_ChemClass`, a MED-RT predicate
  DrugCentral does not assert.
- **A canonical pair table plus an `ingest_unresolved_ddi_endpoint` table**, reproducing db/031's ONCHIGH
  shape exactly. Rejected because db/031 needed that second table only for endpoints that were **in no table
  at all**; here the assertion table already holds every row. And it would resolve the 33 duplicates at
  **write** time in Python, discarding the losing row and putting the precedence rule in code — the opposite
  of db/037's standing instruction that the rule choosing between two grades is stated once, in SQL.
- **Mapping the band in the parser and storing only drugref's grade.** Rejected: the upstream label becomes
  unrecoverable without re-reading a 1.4 GB dump, and a clinical judgement lives where no query can show it.
- **Storing no severity at all**, matching MED-RT exactly. Rejected: it discards the only thing this source
  adds, for 7,501 pairs no curator will reach in any foreseeable round.

### 4.2 What is built

One storage table holding every published bundleable row, and views over it. The precedent is `db/039`'s
`fda_cyp_assertion`, whose comment states the principle: *"It holds every tuple the parser produced — members
and withheld alike — because the withheld ones are the point."*

## 5. `db/049_drugcentral_ddi.sql`

### 5.1 Source admission — two CHECKs and one Python entry, in one commit

**Copied verbatim off the live catalog, never retyped** — `db/039`'s comment explains why, and it was written
after a plan's stale list would have dropped `'DRUGREF'`:

```sql
-- READ ON 2026-08-23 FROM drugref_dc101's pg_constraint. THIS SPEC IS NOT THE SOURCE
-- OF THESE LISTS -- re-read them before writing the migration. An elided writer list
-- is deliberate: a spec that carried the whole list would eventually be copied from.
ingest_run_source: ('UNII','CHEBI','MED-RT','MeSH','PBS','DRUGREF','GSRS','ONCHIGH','FDA-CYP')
                 + 'DRUGCENTRAL'
ingest_run_writer: (11 writers, ending 'gsrs_run','onchigh_run','fda_cyp_run')
                 + 'drugcentral_run'
```

`src/drugref/ids.py` gains `"DRUGCENTRAL": "DRUGCENTRAL"` in the same commit, with the comment recording that
the upper-case fall-through was **checked** rather than assumed — the fourth entry added for that reason after
`DRUGREF`, `GSRS` and `FDA-CYP`. A test asserts the pair.

**`class_contraindication_source` is NOT widened, and neither is `moiety_contraindication_source`.**
HANDOVER said the first of these needed widening; that is wrong and is corrected by this design. DrugCentral
writes no class rule and no row into either table. The live CHECKs (`('MED-RT','ONCHIGH')` and `('MED-RT')`)
stay as they are.

### 5.2 `drugref.ddi_source_severity` — the mapping, as data

```sql
CREATE TABLE drugref.ddi_source_severity (
    source       text NOT NULL,
    source_label text NOT NULL,
    severity     text NOT NULL REFERENCES drugref.severity_kind(severity),
    PRIMARY KEY (source, source_label)
);
INSERT INTO drugref.ddi_source_severity VALUES
    ('DRUGCENTRAL', 'Critical',    'contraindicated'),
    ('DRUGCENTRAL', 'Significant', 'moderate');
```

**Why a table and not four lines of Python.** db/006's finding, one tier up: a vocabulary written in code and
in a CHECK is two lists to widen and one way to disagree. Here the mapping is additionally a **clinical
judgement**, and a clinical judgement drugref makes on a consumer's behalf must be queryable — a node operator
must be able to `SELECT` it, disagree with it, and see exactly what it did. Revising it is then a migration
over two rows, not a re-ingest of 7,571.

**The mapping itself.** VA/NDF-RT's own semantics are *Critical = avoid the combination* and *Significant =
may have clinical consequences; monitor or adjust*, so each band maps to the drugref grade that says the same
thing. Sampled evidence for `Critical`: `cisapride + miconazole`, `azithromycin + pimozide`,
`isocarboxazid + dextropropoxyphene`, `posaconazole + simvastatin`. For `Significant`:
`erythromycin + repaglinide`, `glimepiride + ciprofloxacin`, `apixaban + heparin`.

**`major` is deliberately unused by this source, and that is a signal rather than an omission.** A two-band
authority has two bands. Spreading them across three grades would invent a distinction VA does not draw. The
cost is stated rather than hidden: some `Significant` pairs (`fluvoxamine + tapentadol`, `apixaban + heparin`)
are arguably major and are graded a notch low — which is what the curated overlay exists to correct, one pair
at a time, and what makes the mapping's revisability load-bearing.

### 5.3 `drugref.drugcentral_ddi_assertion` — every bundleable row, as published

A rebuildable projection keyed by `ingest_run.source`, so a per-source rebuild is delete-and-rebuild like
every other ingested feed.

```sql
CREATE TABLE drugref.drugcentral_ddi_assertion (
    ingest_run      bigint NOT NULL REFERENCES drugref.ingest_run(ingest_run_id),
    source          text   NOT NULL CHECK (source = 'DRUGCENTRAL'),
    upstream_key    text   NOT NULL,
    endpoint_1_name text   NOT NULL,
    endpoint_2_name text   NOT NULL,
    upstream_label  text   NOT NULL,
    severity_label  text   NOT NULL,
    moiety_1_uuid   uuid   REFERENCES drugref.substance_moiety(moiety_uuid),
    moiety_2_uuid   uuid   REFERENCES drugref.substance_moiety(moiety_uuid),
    route_1         text   NOT NULL,
    route_2         text   NOT NULL,
    PRIMARY KEY (ingest_run, source, upstream_key),
    FOREIGN KEY (source, severity_label)
        REFERENCES drugref.ddi_source_severity(source, source_label),
    CONSTRAINT drugcentral_ddi_assertion_route_1 CHECK (route_1 IN
        ('display_name','inchikey','cas',
         'not_a_substance','no_structural_key','missing_keys_row','unresolved')),
    CONSTRAINT drugcentral_ddi_assertion_route_2 CHECK (route_2 IN
        ('display_name','inchikey','cas',
         'not_a_substance','no_structural_key','missing_keys_row','unresolved')),
    CONSTRAINT drugcentral_ddi_assertion_endpoint_1_complete
        CHECK ((route_1 IN ('display_name','inchikey','cas')) = (moiety_1_uuid IS NOT NULL)),
    CONSTRAINT drugcentral_ddi_assertion_endpoint_2_complete
        CHECK ((route_2 IN ('display_name','inchikey','cas')) = (moiety_2_uuid IS NOT NULL))
);
```

**Seven routes, not four.** `drugcentral_resolve` already holds the closed vocabulary as three frozensets —
`RESOLVED_ROUTES` (3), `UNRESOLVED_ROUTES` (4), `ROUTES` (their union) — and `missing_keys_row` is one of
them: a `struct_id` present in the name index and absent from the key index, which cannot happen on a
well-formed extract and is counted apart precisely so *a corrupt extract does not pass for a difficult one*.
The two CHECKs above restate `ROUTES` and `RESOLVED_ROUTES` in SQL, so this is a vocabulary in two homes —
the defect db/006 was written to remove. **It is admitted deliberately and pinned by a test** that reads the
live CHECK definitions out of `pg_constraint` and asserts their admitted sets equal the two frozensets. That
is the same discipline `ids._SOURCE_CANONICAL` and `ingest_run_source` already live under: *"the two are a
pair, and a test asserts both."* A lookup table the column FKs into was considered and rejected — a
descriptive column that nothing joins on does not earn a table, and the FK would not catch the drift the test
catches (a route removed from Python while the CHECK still admits it).

Four column decisions, each of which was a choice:

- **`upstream_key` is `ddi.source_id`** (the VA's own record key, `'C56^4966^'`), **not `ddi.id`.** Measured:
  all 7,571 bundleable rows carry a distinct `source_id`, so it is a valid key — and it is the upstream
  *authority's* identifier rather than an artifact of one dump's row numbering. A dump row number in a key a
  gap could ever be built from would not survive a re-publication.
- **`upstream_label` is stored** although §3.1 shows it carries no clinical content, because it is the only
  visible record of the salt grain that produces §3.2's 33 duplicates. Without it, a reader who finds one pair
  twice has no way to see why.
- **The severity FK is the load-bearing constraint.** A future release inventing a third band is **refused at
  INSERT**, loudly, rather than stored and silently mapped to nothing. This is db/006's lesson applied to a
  vocabulary that crosses a source boundary.
- **The two completeness CHECKs** make *"resolved but no uuid"* and *"a uuid on an unresolved route"*
  unrepresentable rather than merely discouraged — `curated_interaction_ruling_is_complete`'s shape.

**Self-pairs are permitted in this table and excluded by the view.** Two endpoint names folding onto one
moiety is a consequence of resolution, not a malformed row; db/014's `moiety_contraindication_not_self` guards
a table where it *would* be malformed. The count is 0 today and the summary reports it as its own bucket, so
it cannot become nonzero unnoticed.

### 5.4 `drugref.drugcentral_ddi_pair` — canonical unordered pairs, graded

```sql
CREATE VIEW drugref.drugcentral_ddi_pair AS
SELECT DISTINCT ON (p.moiety_lo, p.moiety_hi)
       p.moiety_lo, p.moiety_hi, p.source AS candidate_source,
       m.severity, s.severity_rank, p.severity_label AS upstream_severity_label,
       p.upstream_key, p.upstream_label, p.ingest_run,
       r.upstream_release, r.finished_at AS ingested_at
FROM  (SELECT least(a.moiety_1_uuid, a.moiety_2_uuid)    AS moiety_lo,
              greatest(a.moiety_1_uuid, a.moiety_2_uuid) AS moiety_hi, a.*
       FROM drugref.drugcentral_ddi_assertion a
       WHERE a.moiety_1_uuid IS NOT NULL AND a.moiety_2_uuid IS NOT NULL
         AND a.moiety_1_uuid <> a.moiety_2_uuid) p
JOIN  drugref.ddi_source_severity m ON m.source = p.source AND m.source_label = p.severity_label
JOIN  drugref.severity_kind       s ON s.severity = m.severity
JOIN  drugref.ingest_run          r ON r.ingest_run_id = p.ingest_run
ORDER BY p.moiety_lo, p.moiety_hi, s.severity_rank, p.upstream_key;
```

`DISTINCT ON` + `ORDER BY … severity_rank` is **most-severe-wins**, and it needs no `DESC` because db/035 made
rank 1 the most severe precisely so the safe read is the one a caller writes by default. The 4 self-disagreeing
pairs of §3.2 therefore surface as `contraindicated`. The rule is stated **once, here** — a consumer querying
from any language gets it.

**`p.upstream_key` is the final sort key and it is load-bearing, not decoration.** Two rows can tie on
`severity_rank` — 29 of the 33 duplicate pairs do — and `DISTINCT ON` keeps the first row of a group, so
without a total order the *reported* `upstream_key` and `upstream_label` could differ between two runs over
the same bytes. That is precisely the defect the re-measurement's own review found in three registry lookups
that had no `ORDER BY`, in the round whose entire justification was reproducibility. A test pins it.

### 5.5 `drugref.exact_ddi_pair` — the read path drugref has never had

```sql
moiety_lo, moiety_hi          -- always populated; the unordered lookup key
subject_moiety, object_moiety -- populated ONLY where the source asserts a direction
candidate_source, relationship
severity, severity_rank, upstream_severity_label   -- NULL where the source asserts no grade
upstream_release, ingested_at
```

Arm 1 is `moiety_contraindication` — MED-RT's exact pairs (**1,442** at slice 5b's measurement, re-derived in
§9 rather than quoted forward) — which reach **no consumer view today**:
`ddi_candidate_pair` expands `class_contraindication` only, and nothing else reads that table. Arm 2 is
`drugcentral_ddi_pair`. `severity` is NULL on arm 1 because MED-RT asserts none, and `subject_moiety` is NULL
on arm 2 because DrugCentral asserts no direction — each NULL states a fact about its source rather than
hiding a missing value.

## 6. Why `ddi_candidate_pair` is not touched

The harm-direction argument for unioning into it is real: a consumer who queries the view they already know
gets silence on a pair DrugCentral grades `contraindicated`. It is outweighed by two measured facts.

- **db/034 measured this exact move costing 3.6× with the new arm EMPTY** — a structural cost paid by every
  existing consumer on every query, for content most of them do not have.
- `ddi_candidate_pair`'s columns are class-expansion-shaped (`via_class`, `member_class`, `is_direct`), all
  meaningless at moiety grain. 7,501 rows of NULLs in three columns is not a read path; it is a second
  vocabulary hiding in a view.

`exact_ddi_pair` is therefore additive: **no existing query changes**, and the new view must still be measured
on the hot path before it ships (§9). The cost is stated plainly: a consumer must learn one view name. That is
recorded in the published decision record so it is discoverable outside this spec.

## 7. The gap — kind 17, `unresolved_ddi_endpoint`

`gap_unresolved_ddi_endpoint` reads the assertion table directly; no third table is needed. Grain is **one
folded endpoint name** — 10 today, from 37 rows — because a curator resolves a name, not a row.

```
gap_key = 'DRUGCENTRAL:ENDPOINT:' || <folded name>
```

Two decisions:

- **Filtered on `moiety_uuid IS NULL`, never on the route vocabulary.** The routes are descriptive; filtering
  on them would put that list in a second place, which is the defect db/006 exists to remove.
- **The name is folded (lower-cased) in the key.** `question_uuid` is immortal and externally cited, so two
  spellings of one endpoint must not mint two questions. The folding is the resolver's own grain.

The 10 all came back on route `unresolved` — DrugCentral holds a structural key and drugref does not — rather
than `no_structural_key`, so **an answer could change something**, which is db/012's test for whether the
review gate may ask at all. `phytomenadione` is the INN for phytonadione, `glycopyrronium bromide` for
glycopyrrolate, `atracurium` for the besylate drugref carries. They are registry-coverage work, and the
question retires by itself when the claim lands.

## 8. Code

| module | kind | job |
|---|---|---|
| `src/drugref/ingest/drugcentral_dump.py` | pure, **moved from `tools/`** | streaming COPY-block reader over the gzipped dump |
| `src/drugref/ingest/drugcentral_resolve.py` | pure, **moved from `tools/`** | the `display_name → inchikey → cas` cascade |
| `src/drugref/ingest/drugcentral.py` | pure | the reference guard, the rule-6 filter, row → assertion record |
| `src/drugref/ingest/drugcentral_run.py` | orchestrator | owns the transaction; loads registry lookups; the only writer |
| `src/drugref/interactions.py` | writer | gains the row writer (383 lines today, and its docstring already claims sole ownership of the interaction tables) |
| `src/drugref/cli_drugcentral.py` | CLI | `drugref ingest drugcentral --release 11012023 --dump …` |

**The move out of `tools/` is the point, not tidying.** Writing a second dump reader and a second cascade for
the ingest would put "what a `ddi` row means" in two homes — and this project has lost six rounds to one rule
kept in two places. Both modules are already pure and test-covered; `tools/` imports them from their new home,
so the instrument that produced §3's figures and the ingest that acts on them are the same code. No
re-export shims: a shim is a third home.

**A standalone subcommand, not a chain step** — following `FDA-CYP`, the most recent content source, rather
than `ONCHIGH`. The dump is 1.4 GB and pinned to a single 2023 release; the chain is the routine
rebuild-everything path. The data dependency is real and documented on the subcommand: the cascade needs
slice-1 identity claims, so `unii` (and `chebi`, for InChIKeys) must have run.

`ingest_run.source_checksum` receives the dump's SHA-256 from the existing `ingest/checksum.py` — so the
anchor the 2026-08-13 run never recorded is captured by the ingest for free, on every run.

**The summary reconciles.** `DrugCentralSummary` carries `rows_read`, `rows_excluded_by_reference`,
`rows_bundleable`, `rows_resolved`, `rows_self_pair`, `rows_unresolved` and `pairs`, and a test asserts
`rows_bundleable = rows_resolved + rows_self_pair + rows_unresolved`. Issue 71's standing rule, and the
`Measurement` guard the re-measurement round added after `self_pair_rows` was computed and rendered nowhere.

## 9. Tests and measurement

TDD throughout: the failing test first, then the code.

- **Fixture.** `tests/fixtures/drugcentral_ddi_subset.sql.gz`, built by `tools/make_drugcentral_subset.py`,
  carrying rows from **all three** references so the rule-6 filter and the §2 reference guard are both
  exercised, plus the `structures`/`synonyms` rows the cascade needs. **The `ddi_ref_id` 1 and 3 descriptions
  are redacted**, following the MED-RT fixture's endpoint redaction, which a test enforces there and will here.
- **Unit tests, no database:** the reference guard aborts on a renumbered reference; the rule-6 filter admits
  only ref 2; a blank structural key is never looked up (the registry-collapse guard, re-asserted at the new
  home); the cascade's route labels; the summary reconciliation.
- **DB-gated tests:** the two `ingest_run` CHECKs admit `DRUGCENTRAL`/`drugcentral_run` and still refuse a
  typo; the severity FK refuses an unmapped band; each completeness CHECK refuses its half; **the two route
  CHECKs admit exactly `drugcentral_resolve.ROUTES` and the completeness CHECKs exactly `RESOLVED_ROUTES`,
  read out of `pg_constraint`**; the pair view collapses a both-order pair to the more severe grade **and is
  stable when the two tie**; `exact_ddi_pair` returns MED-RT rows with NULL severity and DrugCentral rows with
  NULL subject; the gap view yields one row per folded name; a per-source rebuild deletes and re-derives.
- **Measured end to end** on a `TEMPLATE` copy of `drugref_dc101` (`db/048` plus the full documented chain),
  recording: every summary counter; pairs written; the gap-17 count; the `open_question` delta reconciled at
  row level; `moiety_contraindication`'s own row count, re-derived rather than quoted; `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` on
  `exact_ddi_pair`; and proof that **`ddi_candidate_pair` (21,664) and `substance_moiety` (19,438) did not
  move.**

## 10. What this slice does not answer

- **The 2023 pin.** `upstream_release = '11012023'` is honest provenance for a floor that does not refresh.
  `drugcentral.org/download` still offers no successor as of 2026-08-23.
- **Cross-source disagreement at the pair grain.** 635 of the 7,501 pairs are already reachable through
  MED-RT's class expansion, and nothing compares the two. That is [#97](https://github.com/cairn-ehr/drugref/issues/97)/[#106](https://github.com/cairn-ehr/drugref/issues/106)'s
  question one tier down, and this slice adds a third population to it without answering it. It is worth a
  filed issue at the end of the round rather than a silent widening.
- **`major` carries no DrugCentral row.** §5.2 argues that is correct; a curator disagreeing corrects it two
  rows at a time.
